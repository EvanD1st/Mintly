Authorize one exact mint

These approvals authorize spending for one mint. It is different from wallet linking. Never enter a recovery phrase or private key. Your account must already be a compatible MetaMask Smart Account; Mintly will not upgrade it.

The review shows how gas is paid. With direct wallet gas, your main wallet pays actual network gas up to the approved ceiling; failed or prematurely submitted operations can still consume gas. Mintly does not hold your wallet key. The legacy relayer mode uses the fixed reimbursement shown in its review. This permission is valid for at most 30 minutes from its scheduled start and allows only the exact mint. Direct wallet gas also requires a second signature binding the operation and gas ceiling. Future public stages can be authorized up to 24 hours before opening. Cancelling in Mintly stops its worker; a signed permission remains valid on-chain until expiry unless you revoke it in your wallet. Submitted transactions cannot be cancelled here.
