Authorize one exact mint

This signature authorizes spending for one mint. It is different from wallet linking. Never enter a recovery phrase or private key. Your account must already be a compatible MetaMask Smart Account; Mintly will not upgrade it.

You pay the fixed gas reimbursement shown above, included in the total. The operator fronts network gas. This permission is valid for at most 30 minutes from its scheduled start and allows only the exact mint and fee payment together. Future public stages can be authorized up to 24 hours before opening. Cancelling in Mintly stops its worker; a signed permission remains valid on-chain until expiry unless you revoke it in your wallet. Submitted transactions cannot be cancelled here.
