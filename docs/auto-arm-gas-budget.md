# Auto-Arming gas budgets

ETH-denominated Auto-Arming transactions have an execution ceiling of 0.00002 ETH. Smaller owner-approved limits remain smaller. This applies independently in the signer to unsigned existing tasks as well as newly materialized tasks. Immutable older approvals and task snapshots are not rewritten. APE and HYPE keep their separate native-currency limits and dollar-based queued reservation rules. Manual automatic mints and followed copy approvals are unchanged.

Queued ETH Auto-Arming holds remain at most 0.00001 ETH. At execution the signer quotes gas for the final wallet quantity and calldata. The existing quote includes 20% gas-unit headroom, Nitro price headroom and OP-stack parent/operator fee bounds. No extra blanket multiplier is added. If the buffered fee exceeds the effective ceiling, signing waits within existing retries and expiry; the message says gas is above the Auto-Arming limit. Gas estimates are not an actual charge, and protocol parent fees can change before inclusion.

Only the buffered transaction liability is funded and charged to the API reservation ledger, the independently checked daily budget and the signer journal. All limit checks finish before signing. Earlier-opening priority and yielding of later unsigned holds remain intact. A nonce wait or rejected quote leaves the queued hold untouched.

The private journal remains authoritative after a lost database commit. Replays and disabling automation restore the recorded liability without re-quoting or shrinking it. Already signed transactions retain their recorded exposure. A same-nonce recovery reserves the larger of the original and replacement liabilities, with policy and daily limits checked independently; it never counts two same-nonce transactions as two spends. A replacement must also fit the current effective fee ceiling.

Task API fields add effective_fee_cap_eth, reserved_now_eth and actual_total_cost_eth. Existing fee_cap_eth and immutable authorization snapshots retain their audit meaning. Mint Plans labels the effective gas ceiling, current reservation and actual spend separately. Approval responses expose the effective cap separately from the original signed scope; retries of older registering approvals retain their original request payload.

Tests cover exact ceiling boundaries, smaller approvals, unchanged APE/HYPE/manual limits, a legacy unsigned approval rejected above the new ceiling, quotes fitting less than a full policy/daily approval, journal recovery, disabling after commit loss, same-nonce recovery and clear UI labels. The legacy high-fee local EVM fixture uses its previous cap so its quantity and recovery tests keep their original scope; dedicated tests assert the production ceiling and signer enforcement.
